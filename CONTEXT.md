# Siam Si Nacho (เซียมซีนาโช่)

A เซียมซี (Siam Si) fortune-stick game for team Nacho Show Pow(er)'s nacho project: drawing a numbered stick tells you which ingredient goes on your nachos.

## Language

### The game

**Siam Si** (เซียมซี):
The cup of numbered sticks that gets shaken until one stick rises out.
_Avoid_: gacha, gacha machine, cylinder

**Stick** (ไม้เซียมซี):
One numbered stick from the Siam Si; there is one Stick number per Ingredient, and a number always maps to the same Ingredient.
_Avoid_: number, ticket, roll

**Ingredient** (วัตถุดิบ):
The nacho ingredient a Stick number gives you.
_Avoid_: thing, prize, reward, item

**Fortune** (คำทำนาย):
The meaning an Ingredient carries, like the prediction on a real เซียมซี slip; each Fortune has one topic (love, health, work…).
_Avoid_: meaning, message, prediction

**Slip** (ใบเซียมซี):
The reveal for one Stick number, showing its Ingredient and that Ingredient's Fortune; identical whether reached by Pull or by Scan.
_Avoid_: result page, ingredient page

### The two paths

**Shake** (เขย่า):
Shaking the Siam Si until one Stick, chosen with every number equally likely, rises above the others. Only exists on the homepage.
_Avoid_: gacha, roll, spin

**Pull** (ดึง):
Drawing the risen Stick out of the Siam Si, which reveals its number and opens its Slip.
_Avoid_: draw, reveal, open

**Scan** (สแกน):
Opening a Slip directly from the QR code on a real Stick at the booth, skipping Shake and Pull.
_Avoid_: QR mode, direct link
